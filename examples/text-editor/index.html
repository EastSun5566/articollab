Two writers, one live document.

Two CodeMirror editors share one authoritative document through articollab/sync. Edits are sent as you type and the other writer receives them over a push channel; there is no Sync button.

Turn a writer Offline to keep editing without a network: its edits wait, and are sent when it comes back. Shared cursors follow the text they point at; a cursor in text this writer has not received yet is listed below the editor instead of drawn.

This localhost prototype has no authentication, persistence or reload recovery. Inspect checkpoints to see pending work.
