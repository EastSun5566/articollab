Two writers, one board.

Each card is a List element. Drag cards between columns or use their buttons; edits are sent as you make them and the other writer receives them over a push channel. Select a card to rename it.

A move deletes the card and inserts it again, so the server applies a small policy: when two writers move the same card, the first move wins, and a rename aimed at a card someone else moved follows it. A rename of a card someone else deleted is not saved, and the writer is told. Turn a writer Offline to try these races.

This localhost prototype has no authentication, persistence or reload recovery.
